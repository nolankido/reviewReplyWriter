# A real operator trial, not a testimonial template

This protocol is optional for the official Week 2 entry. It is intended to test
the Side Hustle hypothesis: would a real cafe, shop or accommodation operator
find this workflow useful enough to adopt or pay for? No results are assumed.

## Before the session

Ask an operator for permission to try the local app. Use fictional reviews first,
or reviews the operator is authorized to process. Do not copy customer identities,
private policies or session tokens into a public submission. Explain that replies
are drafts, checks are heuristic, and nothing is posted automatically.

Show how to launch the browser workspace. Ask the operator to add a business name
and only offers they actually authorize. Do not pre-check authorization boxes.
Use [the setup guide](LOCAL_SETUP.md) to verify the target computer first. An
installation check and a completed Gemma smoke test are different observations.
If setup fails, record that outcome instead of reporting an AI trial. Templates
can still support a separate trial of the owner review and approval workflow.

## Observe, do not coach every click

Ask the operator to draft one positive, one negative and one mixed response.
Let them read, edit, approve and copy a reply. Record whether they found the
controls, what was confusing, which details were omitted, what wording they
changed, and whether they understood template versus Gemma output. A task that
cannot be completed is a useful result, not a reason to replace the participant.

If measuring time, record start/end definitions and the actual elapsed values.
Do not count model generation time as total work saved. Compare against the
operator's normal method using comparable reviews and describe the limitations.

## Questions to ask afterwards

Which response would you actually post after review, and why? What would prevent
you from using this? Would you pay for setup or support, or would you prefer a
hosted service? What is the most important change? Do not lead with a price or
invent willingness to pay from polite encouragement.

## Record only what happened

Record the date, operator role, device, model, selected scenarios, completion
results, required edits and permission to quote. Leave unknown fields blank.
Distinguish the operator's words from your interpretation. A draft quote must be
approved by the operator before publication. Declining to pay is still evidence.

Quality Lab assessments are diagnostic judgments on fictional fixtures. They
are not customer adoption, publication approval or independent endorsements.
Nothing in Submission Desk inserts a first-user story automatically.
